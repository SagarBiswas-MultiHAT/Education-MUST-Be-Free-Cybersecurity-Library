# ⚔️ Penetration Testing & Red Team Methodology

Comprehensive roadmap for offensive security assessments, infrastructure testing, and red team engagement strategies.

---

## 📚 Curated Book Sequence

### 🟢 Beginner Level

#### 1. The Basics of Hacking and Penetration Testing by Patrick Engebretson
- **Focus**: Core phases of penetration testing (Recon, Scanning, Exploitation, Reporting).
- **Recommended Labs**: [TryHackMe Complete Beginner Path](https://tryhackme.com)

#### 2. Coding for Penetration Testers by Jason Andress & Ryan Linn
- **Focus**: Writing custom scripts in Python, Bash, and PowerShell for automation.

---

### 🟡 Intermediate Level

#### 3. Metasploit: The Penetration Tester's Guide by David Kennedy et al.
- **Focus**: Mastering the Metasploit framework, custom module development, and post-exploitation.

#### 4. The Hacker Playbook 3: Practical Guide to Penetration Testing by Peter Kim
- **Focus**: Red team tactics, adversary emulation, lateral movement, and privilege escalation.

---

### 🔴 Advanced Level

#### 5. Advanced Penetration Testing by Wiley
- **Focus**: Evasion techniques, custom C2 infrastructure, complex enterprise domain compromise.
